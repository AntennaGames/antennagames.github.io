Gameplay Analytics

This game collects limited gameplay analytics to help us understand how players interact with the game and to improve the game's levels, difficulty, balance, performance, and overall experience. If you allow analytics collection, the following information may be sent to and stored on our analytics server: Player ID — a randomly generated identifier used to associate gameplay events with the same player and link gameplay across multiple sessions for progression and improvement analysis. It is not intended to directly identify you by name or contact information. Session ID — an identifier used to distinguish individual game sessions. Event type — the type of gameplay event that occurred, such as starting a level, completing a level, dying, or quitting the game. Level — the game level associated with the event. Session time — the amount of time played during the current game session. Level time — the amount of time spent on the relevant level. Game version — the version of the game being played. The game does not use this analytics system to collect your name, email address, home address, or other directly identifying contact information. Why We Collect This Data We use gameplay analytics to understand how the game is played. This may include analyzing: How many players start and complete each level. Where players frequently die or quit. How long players spend playing the game. How long players spend on individual levels. How players progress through the game. Whether changes to the game affect player behavior. This information is used to improve and develop the game.

Legal Basis

Where analytics collection is based on your consent, analytics data is collected only after you choose to allow analytics collection. You can choose not to allow analytics collection. Choosing not to allow analytics does not prevent you from playing the game. You can also withdraw your consent through the available analytics/privacy settings in the game.

Data Storage

Analytics data is stored on our server/database and is accessible to the game developer and authorized service providers who may be involved in hosting or operating the game's online services. Gameplay analytics data is stored on a server provided by Wispbyte, using a MySQL database hosted on the same service. The data is stored and processed as part of the technical infrastructure used to operate the game's online services. Wispbyte may process or have access to data as necessary to provide and maintain its hosting services. For more information about how Wispbyte handles data and privacy, please refer to Wispbyte's current privacy policy and terms of service.

Data Retention

We retain gameplay analytics only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy. Analytics data is retained for 24 months and is then deleted.

Data Sharing

We do not sell gameplay analytics data. Gameplay analytics may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate our server, database, or other technical infrastructure. These providers only receive data as necessary to provide those services.
